Questions, Answered

A few things guests often ask before they arrive. For anything else, our team is only a call away.

Where is Ashvana, and how do I reach it? +

Avantra sits in the hills of Sakleshpur, deep in the Malnad region of Karnataka's Western Ghats. It is roughly a four to five hour drive from Bengaluru and about three hours from Mangaluru, whose airport is the nearest. The closest railway station is Sakleshpur. Once you book, our team shares precise directions and can help arrange a transfer.

What is included during my stay? +

Your days here are full without being busy. There is an infinity pool, a multi-cuisine restaurant, forest walking trails, indoor and outdoor games, a children's play area, and a wellness spa. As evening falls, there is a bonfire, an open-air amphitheatre, a poolside snacks counter, and talks led by resident coffee experts.

Is Avantrasuited to families, couples, and groups? +

Yes, to all of them. Intimate cottages hold space for couples, the Family Cottage gathers everyone close, and our banquet hall and amphitheatre make room for celebrations, reunions, and corporate retreats alike.

What is the food like? +

Every meal is drawn from the land around it. Regional Malnad delicacies sit alongside gourmet plates, cooked from the freshest ingredients and served where birdsong and breeze are part of the table.

Can Avantrahost events and corporate retreats? +

It is built for them. The banquet hall, the open-air amphitheatre, and the wide grounds suit weddings, vows, milestone celebrations, and offsites that people remember. Share your plans through the enquiry form and our team will design around them.

What kinds of stays are available? +

Five, each shaped for a different kind of guest. The Twin Cottage suits couples, the Deluxe and Family Cottages open up for those travelling together, the Premium Cottage is our most private retreat, and the Hornbill Suite is a striking container-style stay tucked into the canopy. Every room frames a view and carries the modern comforts you would expect.

When is the best time to visit? +

There is no wrong season. The monsoon, from June to September, turns the hills emerald and wraps the mornings in mist. October to February brings cool, clear days that are perfect for walking and watching the valley. In the drier months, coffee blossom scents the whole estate.

What is there to see nearby? +

Plenty, and all within an easy drive. Manjarabad Fort, the waterfalls at Hadlu and Manjehalli, Bisle View Point, the temples at Kukke Subramanya and Sakleshpura, the Hoysala marvels of Halebidu and Belur, and the sunken church at Shettihalli are close at hand. You will find the full list, with distances, in the Location section above.

How do I book, or ask for something specific? +

Share your dates through the enquiry form or call us at +91 81528 83388, and our team will confirm availability and tailor your stay. For special requests, whether early check-in, accessibility, or pet arrangements, reach out directly and we will do our best to accommodate.
